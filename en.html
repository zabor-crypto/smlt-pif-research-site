Academic research · Financial modelling · Quant engineering

Can a hedge work for the whole portfolio?

A hedge can reduce one exposure and still fail when cash, margin, liquidity and settlement meet a sequence of market events. This academic prototype treats the proposed hedge and the fund's continuing book as one problem.

The result presented here is a financial specification and software prototype. Comparative economic effectiveness, market out-of-sample performance and live readiness have not been established.

What to inspect

Information timing

Inputs are distinguished by what was available at a decision time. Later information must not silently enter an earlier decision.

Feasible execution

A candidate allocation, a recorded intention and a later executed trade are different states. Integer contracts, cash, margin and costs constrain the transition.

A continuing book

Positions and obligations continue through HOLD, rejection and blocked execution. A plausible endpoint is insufficient without the path that reaches it.

Contribution and collaboration

Boris Zabavnikov led the project and quantitative modelling: the financial problem, fund model, hedging and optimization algorithms, and execution mechanism. This was a six-person academic project with shared engineering and testing, alongside issuer, macroeconomic, credit and instrument research.

The team page attributes the individual workstreams. Repository ownership does not imply sole authorship of every component.

A bounded, reproducible entry point

  1. Read the source repository's English brief. It identifies the exported snapshot and excluded material.
  2. Use its Python 3.12 environment and locked dependencies. Run only the documented synthetic data/storage and option-mechanics tests.
  3. Keep the conclusion scoped: these examples check their formulas and mechanics, not the full adaptive policy or its economics.

Market datasets, economic-run configurations, generated research outputs, portraits and original academic documents are not distributed with this site. The full market study cannot be reproduced from this public source export.
